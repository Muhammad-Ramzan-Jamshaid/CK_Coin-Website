How We Protect Our Community

Crypto security begins with proactive defense. Keep these three core safety rules in mind.

🚫

No Team DMs

Verification Rule #1

Admins and founders of CK Coin will never direct message (DM) you first offering customer support, exclusive bonus allocations, or early wallet verification. Any DM asking for funds is an impersonator.

🔑

Never Share Seed Phrases

Verification Rule #2

No legitimate cryptocurrency project or technical representative will ever ask for your private keys, seed phrase, or wallet passcodes. Never connect your wallet to unverified external sites.

🔗

Official Link Verification

Verification Rule #3

Always bookmark ckcoin.community (or confirmed official domain). Verify that every link, contract address, and announcement matches the URLs published on this verified static portal.
